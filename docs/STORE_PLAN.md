# Store Plan

Status: planning draft (SVTLW-96). The Steam store page is NOT created. Creating it needs the owner's permission, and no page, listing or pricing is to be set up until that is given.

## Store page text (draft)

**Short description (under 300 characters)**

> The Last Wall is a dark medieval incremental defence game. Hold the last fortress against endless hordes, grow your stronghold wall by wall, and survive the waves.

**Long description (draft)**

> The last human fortress stands in a world overrun by hostile creatures. Recruit archers, raise walls and production buildings, and watch your stronghold change as you advance. Waves grow larger and harder. Spend what you gather, research what you find, and hold the line.
>
> - Incremental fortress building: every upgrade shows on the battlefield.
> - Defend against large hordes with growing defensive power.
> - Recruit and specialise defenders.
> - Save and reload at any time.
>
> Final art and audio are still in production.

Wording is a draft for the owner to approve. Nothing here has been published.

## Capsule art brief

- Sizes: the main capsule and smaller capsule variants at the sizes the store asks for. Confirm sizes on the store documentation at the time of submission.
- Subject: a single fortress wall under pressure, with one archer silhouette in the foreground. Dark medieval palette from `docs/ART_STANDARDS.md` (stone and wood browns, muted red for danger).
- Title: "The Last Wall" in a readable face, with contrast of at least 4.5:1 against the background.
- No text other than the title. The capsule must read at thumbnail size.
- Commissioned or drawn art must carry a licence entry in `assets/CREDITS.md` before use.

## Trailer outline (6 beats, 60 to 90 seconds)

1. **Hook (0:00 to 0:10).** Dark title card, a single torch, the wall in silhouette.
2. **The last fortress (0:10 to 0:25).** Calm build phase: archers recruited, the stronghold grows.
3. **The horde (0:25 to 0:40).** Waves rolling in; the wall takes damage, state by state.
4. **Growing power (0:40 to 0:55).** Upgrades and specialisations change the fortress on screen.
5. **Hold the line (0:55 to 1:10).** A large wave breaks against the upgraded wall.
6. **Title and call (1:10 to 1:20).** Logo, "Wishlist" line only once the owner has approved a store page.

Footage comes from the build at the M7 quality bar. Until then, the trailer is a plan only.
